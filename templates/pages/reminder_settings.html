How Reminders Work

Reminder rules are checked daily. When an unpaid invoice matches a rule's trigger, an email is sent to the client. Reminders are only sent for invoices in Sent, Viewed, or Overdue status.

{invoice_id} {client_name} {amount} {due_date}

These variables are available in email templates and will be replaced with actual invoice data.
